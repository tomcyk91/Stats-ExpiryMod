PBOX3 FORMAT
============

PBOX3 fields:
0  PBOX3
1  Stats&Expiry persistent GUID
2  Box.BoxID
3  ProductID
4  position.x
5  position.y
6  position.z
7  rotation.x
8  rotation.y
9  rotation.z
10 rotation.w
11 ExpirationDays CSV
12 DeliveryDays CSV

Invariant:
len(ExpirationDays) == len(DeliveryDays) == Box.ProductCount

Game Box.Data.UID is intentionally NOT persisted in this record.

Shelf delivery metadata:
SDEL|DisplaySlotPath|DeliveryDays CSV

Old shelf expiration format remains:
DisplaySlotPath|ExpirationDays CSV
